Checked October 18, 2026

What I found

01

The home page weighs about 12.8 MB and the main photo takes about 30 seconds on a slow-phone simulation.

On two Lighthouse runs (a lab simulation of a phone on a slow connection) the first content showed at 3.7 and 3.6 seconds, but the largest item, the technician photo at the top, didn't finish until 30.1 and 29.8 seconds, and the performance score was 41 and 55. Three images make up most of the weight: the AC repair photo at 4.3 MB, the heating photo at 4.1 MB and your logo at 1.9 MB. Lighthouse estimates about 10.9 MB could be saved with modern formats and right-sized images. That is a lab number, not a measurement of your visitors.

02

Your page code points at a web address that doesn't exist.

The home page carries two canonical links, amrlv.com and actionmaintenancerepair.com, and its business markup and og:url tag use actionmaintenancerepair.com. When I looked that address up on October 18, 2026 it had no DNS record, so it doesn't load. Search engines get two different answers about which address is yours, and Lighthouse flags the canonical as invalid.

03

Other listings disagree with your site.

Your Contact page says Monday to Friday 7am to 7pm and Saturday 8am to 5pm. Your HomeAdvisor profile says Monday to Saturday 8am to 6pm, and says you don't offer free estimates or emergency service, while your site offers “Get a Free Quote” and “Emergency service available”. That profile's website link, actionmaintenanceandrepairlv.com, lands on a parked-domain page instead of your site.

04

Your Services page leaves out two services your form and search description offer.

The search description for the Services page mentions ductwork and smart thermostat services, and the contact form's Service Type list includes Ductwork & Air Quality and Thermostat & Controls. The Services page itself shows four services, AC Repair, Heating Repair, System Installation and Preventive Maintenance, and nothing on ductwork or thermostats.

05

A script error and four vague links on the home page.

Lighthouse logged a browser console error on load (React error #418, a mismatch between what the server sent and what the browser drew), scored SEO 85, and flagged four “Learn More” links that all go to the Services page without saying which service. Accessibility scored 96, with one low-contrast text line in the Comfort Club section. Third-party GoDaddy code blocked the page for about 280 milliseconds in the first run.

06

Your business markup has a phone number but no address, hours or rating.

The business entry has a name, a phone number and a description. It has no street address or service area, no opening hours and no rating, and your pages give no street address either. The “Leave us a review on Google” link on the home page is a good start.

Unmeasured
